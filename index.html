1.2 m/s²

Watch the step signal strip while walking. Each footstep should cross the dashed line once. While you're walking, the line drops for softer steps (slowing down at a door) when they come on the beat, down to 40% of this setting. Lower it if steps are still missed, raise it if one step counts twice.

Corrected view

Walking nearly parallel to the walls is squared up, which also cancels the gyro's slow drift. Diagonal walking keeps its direction.

Lines closer than about 1 m count as the same path. When you turn round (on the spot, on a curve, or after stopping to look), the trace follows your path back. When you walk along an earlier stretch, it snaps onto it and spreads the error over the steps in between. Lower this if separate paths get joined; raise it if a return path still runs beside the old one. On the Live tab it never goes fully off, since joining is how your walk finds the map.

Sensors and data

Both are recorded on every step, so you can switch after a walk and compare. Gyro ignores magnetic interference but slowly drifts. Compass doesn't drift but bends near fridges, steel and wiring.

  • Camera walks (Android): wait at S until it says Tracking, then walk with the phone held up so the camera sees the room, not a blank wall or the floor. Turn slowly. Good light helps.
  • Step walks: hold the phone flat in front of you, screen up, like reading a map.
  • Start at a spot you can find again, like the door, facing along a wall.
  • Walk at a normal, steady pace. Shuffling and very slow steps get missed.
  • When you're back at the start, tap I'm at start. The Corrected view closes the loop there.
  • Tap Mark place each time you stand in a doorway, and use the same names every walk. Each mark pins the map there. Tapped the wrong name? Mark place › Undo last mark.
  • Walk the map two or three times (Walk 2, Walk 3), each from S. The places are put where the walks agree.
  • Live: start at S, facing the way you started the map, or tap Mark place where you are. If your dot drifts off, tap Mark place at the next door.
  • On Android each counted step buzzes, so you can feel a missed one. If steps go missing, step a bit more firmly or lower Step threshold.
  • Keep the screen on. The page gets no motion data while the screen is locked or you’re in another app.
