Corrected view

Walking nearly parallel to the walls is squared up, which also cancels the gyro's slow drift. Diagonal walking keeps its direction.

Lines closer than about 1 m count as the same path. When you turn round (on the spot, on a curve, or after stopping to look), the trace follows your path back. When you walk along an earlier stretch, it snaps onto it and spreads the error over the steps in between. Lower this if separate paths get joined; raise it if a return path still runs beside the old one.

Sensors and data

Both are recorded on every step, so you can switch after a walk and compare. Gyro ignores magnetic interference but slowly drifts. Compass doesn't drift but bends near fridges, steel and wiring.

Export saves a picture and a .json of the walk. Open a .json here to look at it again on any device. The demo is a simulated bedroom walk with everyday sensor errors: both sides of the bed (turning round on the spot at one, on a curve at the other), then back out the door.

  • Hold the phone flat in front of you, screen up, like reading a map.
  • Start at a spot you can find again, like the door, facing along a wall.
  • Walk at a normal, steady pace. Shuffling and very slow steps get missed.
  • When you're back at the start, tap I'm at start. The Corrected view closes the loop there.
  • Keep the screen on. iPhone stops sending motion data when it locks or you switch apps.
